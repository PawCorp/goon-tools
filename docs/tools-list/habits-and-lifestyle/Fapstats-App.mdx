# Fapstats App
**Sex, masturbation and chastity tracker**

Fapstats is an app for tracking sex, masturbation and chastity. It gives you lots of statistics for so many different things like your sex partners or toys. You can unlock achievements, set personal records and more!

> https://app.fapstats.com

## Features

- Track sex and masturbation activities
- Get statistics based on your sexual life
- Create Partners, Toys and custom Tags
- Calendar page for easy overview
- Fucketlist (sexual Bucketlist)
- Chastity Tracker (built in)
- Achievements (standard and custom)
- Records
- XP and Levelsystem with Leaderboard
- No Limits and completely free
- Easy login with Discord or Google

> Read more on our [Website](https://www.fapstats.com)
 
## Compatibility

<Compatibility web android iOS />

- Web based, works on anything with a web browser.
- On your phone, visit the website https://app.fapstats.com, click the browser menu button, and "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app to your phone.
- The Fapstats App will also be released in the Apple App Store and Google Play Store (comming soon)
